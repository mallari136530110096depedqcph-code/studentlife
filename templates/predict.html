How it works

The system estimates a student's grade using Linear Regression. When enough records with actual grades are available, the model fits coefficients to those records. Otherwise, it uses the initial baseline formula.

Estimate = 0.5 × Previous Grade
+ 0.3 × (Study Hours × 10)
+ 0.2 × Attendance
Predictions are estimates for monitoring—not guaranteed final grades.
